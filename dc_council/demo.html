Claim-to-Evidence Binder

When someone at a DC Council meeting cites a bill by number, does that number point to a real legislative record, and does what they say match it? We checked every spoken citation in the meeting captions against the Council’s LIMS records, then tested whether an LLM judge that may answer “can’t tell” makes fewer mistakes than one forced to decide.
